An echocardiogram is an ultrasound of the heart. It shows the size and shape of the heart's chambers, how strongly the muscle squeezes, and how the valves open and close.

It is used to look into heart murmurs, shortness of breath, swollen legs, long-standing high blood pressure, or the heart of people who already have heart disease. We do it for adults and children.

One of the numbers you will see in the report is the **ejection fraction**: the percentage of blood the heart pumps out with each beat. Your doctor will explain what it means in your case.
