A stress test looks at how your heart behaves when it has to work harder. It is used to find the cause of chest pain, to see whether enough blood reaches the heart, and to learn how much exercise you can do safely.

A **stress echocardiogram** adds an ultrasound of the heart before and after the effort. This way the specialist can compare how the muscle moves at rest and when it works harder.

For this test, come with an adult who can make decisions and sign the informed consent. Take your time to read the preparation instructions: they include fasting, avoiding caffeine and reviewing some of your medicines with your doctor.
