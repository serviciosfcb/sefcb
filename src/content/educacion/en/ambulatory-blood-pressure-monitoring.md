Ambulatory blood pressure monitoring (ABPM), also called a blood pressure Holter, measures your blood pressure many times over a full day, including while you sleep.

It is used to confirm whether a person has high blood pressure, to see whether treatment keeps it under control all day long, and to find cases where blood pressure goes up only at the doctor's office, because of nerves during the visit.
