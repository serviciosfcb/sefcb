A Holter monitor records every beat of your heart for hours while you go about your normal life. It is used to look into palpitations, dizziness or fainting, and to keep track of arrhythmias or the effect of some medicines.

Unlike an ECG, which lasts only a few minutes, a Holter monitor can catch changes that happen only once in a while.
