An electrocardiogram (ECG) records your heart's electrical activity with each heartbeat. With it, your doctor can see whether the rhythm is regular, how fast your heart is beating, and whether there are signs that part of the heart muscle is not getting enough blood.

It is one of the most common tests in cardiology because it is quick, doesn't hurt and has no risks. We do it for adults and children.

If you have questions about your result after the test, see how to get your [results and medical records](/en/patients/results-and-medical-records/) or ask your doctor at your next visit.
