Nuclear medicine shows how organs work, not only what they look like. That is why it complements radiology, which defines anatomy. It is a multidisciplinary specialty that draws on radiopharmacy, medical physics and computing.

Our new dual-head gamma camera makes image acquisition faster.

## Benefits

- It is highly sensitive and provides information other methods cannot.
- It can detect disease at early stages, before symptoms appear.
- It allows an early assessment of the response to chemotherapy and radiotherapy.
- The procedures are safe and minimally invasive.
- It often avoids unnecessary or more costly procedures.
