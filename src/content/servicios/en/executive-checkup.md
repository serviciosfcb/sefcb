The executive cardiovascular checkup brings together, in a single day, a visit with the cardiologist, lab tests and the heart studies that show your cardiovascular and metabolic risk. At the end, a doctor reviews all your results together and explains them to you, in person or by video.

Knowing your heart today is caring for your life tomorrow. Choose the package that suits you best.

**Exclusive benefit:** if your specialist decides that you need advanced tests, you will get a 20% preferential discount.

The day includes time for nature immersion and wellness. Learn why contact with nature is good for you in [Vitamin N](/en/patients/health-education/vitamin-n/).
