title: Non-invasive cardiology
tag: Adults and children
summary: Heart tests without surgery, to diagnose early and plan your treatment.
order: 1

groups:
  - title: Tests
    items:
      - name: Electrocardiogram (ECG)
        detail: Records the heart's electrical activity with every beat. Adults and children.
      - name: Transthoracic echocardiogram
        detail: An ultrasound that shows the shape and function of the heart. Adults and children.
      - name: Stress echocardiogram, with exercise or medication
        detail: Images of the heart in motion while it works harder. Adults and children.
      - name: Transesophageal echocardiogram
        detail: Combines ultrasound with a probe passed through the esophagus for a more detailed view of the heart. Adults.
      - name: Holter monitoring
        detail: Continuously records heart rate and rhythm over a period of time. Adults and children.
      - name: Ambulatory blood pressure monitoring
        detail: Continuously records your blood pressure while you go about your day. Adults and children.
      - name: Exercise stress test
        detail: Assesses how the heart responds to progressive physical exercise. Adults and children.
      - name: Tilt table test
        detail: Looks for the cause of fainting (syncope). Adults and children.

Our non-invasive cardiology unit brings together an interdisciplinary team of specialists who care for people with diseases of the heart and great vessels, with processes focused on patient safety.

With more than ten years of experience, we are a regional referral center thanks to our specialists and up-to-date equipment.
