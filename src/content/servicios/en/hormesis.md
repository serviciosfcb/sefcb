HORMESIS is the obesity program of Servicios Especializados del Corazón. It is part of our prevention and rehabilitation unit, with an interdisciplinary team of sports medicine, nutrition, psychology, physical therapy and nursing.

Excess weight raises the risk of high blood pressure and heart disease. That is why the program combines medical assessment, nutrition, exercise and emotional support.

To find out whether the program is right for you, book an assessment.
