groups:
  - title: Cardiac catheterization and coronary intervention
    intro: Studies the heart's arteries with thin catheters inserted through the groin or the arm. When there is a blockage, the artery can be opened and stents implanted to restore blood flow.
    items:
      - name: Right and left heart catheterization
      - name: Coronary angiography
      - name: Coronary angioplasty with stents, one or more vessels
      - name: Coronary atherectomy
  - title: Electrophysiology
    intro: Studies the heart's electrical activity to assess and treat complex arrhythmias, explain symptoms and estimate the risk of future arrhythmias.
    items:
      - name: Electrophysiology study
      - name: Radiofrequency catheter ablation
      - name: Single- and dual-chamber pacemaker implantation
      - name: Implantable cardioverter-defibrillator (ICD)
      - name: Temporary pacemaker and event monitor implantation
      - name: Device check, reprogramming and removal
  - title: Neurointervention
    intro: Endovascular treatment of diseases of the brain, spinal cord, spine, and head and neck.
    items:
      - name: Cerebral angiography
      - name: Endovascular treatment of aneurysms, malformations and arteriovenous fistulas
      - name: Thrombolysis for ischemic stroke
      - name: Intracranial angioplasty
      - name: Preoperative tumor embolization
  - title: Peripheral vascular
    intro: Diagnosis and treatment of blocked or narrowed arteries and veins in the arms, legs, abdomen and neck.
    items:
      - name: Angiography of the limbs, carotid and renal arteries, and aorta
      - name: Balloon or stent angioplasty of the limbs and abdominal vessels
      - name: Phlebography, venography and cavography
---
Our interventional unit provides invasive diagnosis and treatment in four areas: cardiovascular, neurology, peripheral vascular and electrophysiology. Our technology allows safe, reliable procedures, so patients do not need to travel to other cities.
