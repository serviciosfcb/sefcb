In accordance with articles 378 to 381 of Colombia's Tax Statute, income tax and ICA withholding certificates are available.

You can pick them up at our offices at Calle 19 No. 1-44, Barrio Blanco, Cúcuta, or request them by email at contabilidad@sefcb.co.
