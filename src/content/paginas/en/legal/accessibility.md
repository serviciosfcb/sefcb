This site aims to meet level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.2) and Colombia's NTC 5854 standard.

## Options to adapt the page

With the **Accessibility** button, at the bottom left or at the top of the page, you can:

- Increase the text size in two steps.
- Turn on high contrast.
- Underline all links.
- Increase line spacing.
- Stop all animations.
- Listen to the page read aloud.

Your settings are saved on your device. The site also respects the reduce-motion setting on your phone or computer.

## Other features

- The whole site can be used with a keyboard, and the focus is always visible.
- It works with screen readers.
- It uses a typeface designed for people with low vision.
- It is available in Spanish and English.

## Deaf and hard-of-hearing visitors

You can reach us through Colombia's [Centro de Relevo](https://centroderelevo.gov.co/), a free service of the Ministry of ICT that interprets between Colombian Sign Language and voice.

## Found a barrier?

If something on the site does not work for you, write to contacto@sefcb.co telling us which page and what happened. We will fix it.
