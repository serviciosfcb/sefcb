## 1. Purpose

To set out the guidelines for processing and protecting the personal data that Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. collects, stores, uses, transmits, transfers and, where applicable, deletes, ensuring respect for the rights of data subjects, the confidentiality and security of the information, and compliance with current Colombian personal data protection law.

This policy aims to inform data subjects clearly and transparently about the purposes for which their personal data is processed, the rights they have and the mechanisms the institution has established for exercising them, with special protection for sensitive data related to the provision of health services.

## 2. Scope

This Personal Data Processing and Protection Policy applies to the processing of the personal data of patients, users, legal representatives, relatives and companions where applicable, workers, job applicants, contractors, suppliers, students, visitors and any other natural persons whose personal data is processed by Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. as Data Controller.

It covers the collection, storage, use, consultation, updating, circulation, transmission, transfer, retention and deletion of personal data, by physical, electronic or digital means, in the course of the institution's healthcare, administrative, employment, contractual, commercial, academic and research activities, in accordance with the purposes communicated to data subjects and the applicable legal provisions.

This policy includes the processing of sensitive personal data, especially health-related data, the information contained in medical records and biometric data, when processed by the institution, with the protection and confidentiality measures required by current law.

Its provisions must be followed by the workers and collaborators of Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. and by third parties that process personal data on the institution's behalf, in accordance with their legal and contractual obligations.

This policy applies without prejudice to the special legal regimes that govern certain types of information, particularly medical records, professional secrecy and information subject to legal confidentiality.

## 3. Definitions

For the purposes of this Personal Data Processing and Protection Policy, the following definitions apply:

- **Authorization:** the data subject's prior, express and informed consent to the processing of personal data, when legally required.
- **Database:** an organized set of personal data subject to processing.
- **Personal data:** any information linked or that can be associated with one or more identified or identifiable natural persons.
- **Semi-private personal data:** data that is not intimate, confidential or public, and whose knowledge or disclosure may be of interest to the data subject as well as to a certain sector or group of people or to society in general. Its processing is subject to the applicable legal regime.
- **Sensitive personal data:** data that affects the data subject's privacy or whose misuse could lead to discrimination, such as data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political orientation, religious or philosophical beliefs, or membership in unions or certain types of organizations, as well as data about health, sex life and biometric data.
- **Private personal data:** data that, being intimate or confidential, is relevant only to the data subject and whose processing is subject to the guarantees and conditions set out in the applicable law.
- **Public personal data:** data that is not semi-private, private or sensitive under the applicable law. Examples include data about a person's marital status, profession or trade, and status as a merchant or public servant, as provided by law.
- **Data processor:** a natural or legal person, public or private, that alone or jointly with others processes personal data on behalf of the data controller.
- **Data controller:** a natural or legal person, public or private, that alone or jointly with others decides on the database or the processing of personal data.
- **Data subject:** the natural person whose personal data is processed.
- **Processing:** any operation or set of operations on personal data, such as collection, storage, use, circulation or deletion.
- **Privacy notice:** a verbal or written communication from the controller to the data subject informing them that data processing policies apply to them, how to access those policies and the purposes for which their personal data is processed.
- **Transmission of personal data:** processing that involves communicating personal data within or outside Colombia so that a processor can process it on the controller's behalf.
- **Transfer of personal data:** an operation in which the controller or processor sends personal data to a recipient that in turn acts as a data controller, within or outside Colombia.
- **Medical record:** a private, mandatory and confidential document that records, in chronological order, the patient's health conditions, the medical acts and the other procedures carried out by the healthcare team involved in their care. Third parties may only see it with the patient's authorization or in the cases provided by law.
- **Personal Data Protection Officer:** the person designated by Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. to coordinate, drive and follow up on personal data protection activities and to help data subjects exercise their rights, according to the responsibilities assigned by the institution.
- **Personal data security incident:** an event that compromises or may compromise the confidentiality, integrity or availability of personal data, including unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction.
- **Deletion of personal data:** erasing personal data when legally appropriate, taking into account the purposes of processing, the rights of data subjects and legal obligations to retain information, especially those that apply to medical records.

### 4.2.4. Authorization for the processing of personal data

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. will request and obtain the prior, express and informed authorization of the data subject, or of whoever is legally entitled to give it, when required under current Colombian law.

Authorizations for commercial, advertising, promotional or image-use purposes must clearly identify those purposes and be managed separately from the processing needed to provide healthcare services.

Authorization to process personal data is separate from the informed consent required for health procedures or interventions, although both may be managed through coordinated institutional mechanisms that distinguish their respective scopes.

Data subjects may request revocation of the authorization and deletion of their personal data when legally appropriate, through the institutional channels set out in this policy, subject to the legal and contractual obligations to retain information.

## 4.3. Duties of the data controller

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S., as Personal Data Controller, will fulfill the duties set out in Law 1581 of 2012, its regulations and other applicable rules, guaranteeing the protection of data subjects' rights and the proper processing of their personal information.

- Update information in a timely manner and correct personal data when appropriate, taking the measures needed to maintain its quality.

- Adopt and maintain internal policies, procedures and mechanisms that ensure compliance with personal data protection rules and the proper handling of queries and complaints.
- Allow access to information only to people who are legally or institutionally authorized to process it, according to the corresponding purposes and responsibilities.
- Provide data processors, when applicable, only with personal data whose processing is authorized or legally permitted, and ensure that the information provided is adequate for the intended purposes.
- Require data processors to comply with the applicable security, confidentiality, purpose, restricted-access and other legal and contractual obligations.

- Inform the data subject, upon request, of how their personal data has been used, within the conditions set out in current law.
- Inform the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce of breaches of security codes when there are risks in the management of data subjects' information, in accordance with the applicable legal requirements, conditions and time limits.

- Keep personal data only for the periods needed to fulfill the stated purposes and the applicable legal or contractual obligations, observing the special records retention regimes.
- Implement mechanisms to identify, report, assess, handle and follow up on security incidents that may compromise personal data, taking the containment, correction and notification measures that are legally required.
- Ensure that the transmission, transfer and other forms of communicating personal data to third parties are carried out in accordance with the stated purposes, the required authorizations and the conditions set out in the applicable law.

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. will carry out these duties through the procedures, controls, responsibilities and monitoring mechanisms established in its institutional management system and in its internal documents on personal data protection.

## 4.4. Rights of data subjects

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. recognizes and guarantees the rights of data subjects set out in the Political Constitution of Colombia, Law 1581 of 2012, its regulations and other applicable rules.

Data subjects, personally or through their successors, legal representatives or duly authorized persons, as applicable, may exercise the following rights:

- **Know their personal data:** request information about the personal data that Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. processes.
- **Update their personal data:** request the updating of information that has changed or is out of date, in accordance with the applicable legal requirements.
- **Correct their personal data:** request the correction of data that is partial, inaccurate, incomplete, fragmented or misleading, or whose processing is prohibited or has not been authorized, when applicable.
- **Request proof of authorization:** obtain information and evidence of the authorization given for the processing of their personal data, except in the cases expressly excepted by law.
- **Be informed about the use of their personal data:** request information about the purposes and the processing carried out with their personal data, within the limits set out in the applicable law.
- **Access their personal data free of charge:** consult the personal information that has been processed, under the conditions of free access, frequency and other rules set out in current law.
- **Revoke the authorization:** request revocation of the authorization given for the processing of their personal data, when legally appropriate and provided there is no legal or contractual duty that prevents fully or partially granting the request.
- **Request deletion of personal data:** request the erasure of their personal data when legally appropriate, particularly when the processing does not respect constitutional and legal principles, rights and guarantees, without prejudice to the corresponding retention obligations.
- **Submit queries and complaints:** make requests related to knowing, updating, correcting, deleting, revoking and other rights over their personal data, through the institutional channels established for that purpose.
- **File complaints with the data protection authority:** go to the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce for alleged violations of personal data protection law, once the query or complaint process with the data controller or processor has been completed, in accordance with the law.
- **Decline to provide sensitive data in the cases provided by law:** be informed that answering questions about sensitive personal data or data about children and adolescents is optional, without prejudice to the legal and clinical requirements that apply to the provision of health services.

These rights will be exercised in accordance with the procedures, requirements, time limits and limitations set out in current Colombian law.

When requests relate to medical records, healthcare records, medical information or other documents subject to legal confidentiality, Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. will apply the special provisions on confidentiality, access, custody, retention and protection of the information.

A request to delete personal data or revoke authorization does not automatically erase information that must be kept because of a legal or contractual obligation, including medical records and other records subject to mandatory retention periods.

In these cases, the institution will inform the data subject of the reasons that prevent it from fully or partially granting the request and will limit the processing of the information to the legally permitted purposes.

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. will ensure that data subjects can exercise their rights through the institutional channels set out in this policy, without prejudice to the special mechanisms for accessing medical records and other information subject to legal confidentiality.

## 4.5. Cases in which the data subject's authorization is not required

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S., in accordance with article 10 of Law 1581 of 2012 and other applicable provisions, may process personal data without the data subject's authorization only when one of the exceptions expressly established by law applies.

The data subject's authorization is not required in the following cases:

- **Information required by competent authorities:** when personal data is requested by a public or administrative entity in the exercise of its legal functions or by court order, within the requesting authority's scope of competence and in compliance with the applicable confidentiality provisions.
- **Data of a public nature:** when the processing involves personal data that is public under Colombian law, respecting the principles of purpose, necessity, restricted circulation and other applicable legal conditions.
- **Medical or health emergencies:** when processing personal data is necessary to handle a medical or health emergency, under the conditions provided by law and limited to the information relevant to the situation that gives rise to the exception.
- **Historical, statistical or scientific purposes:** when the processing of information is authorized by law for historical, statistical or scientific purposes, in compliance with the corresponding legal, ethical and personal data protection requirements. In these cases, the necessary measures will be taken to protect the identity of data subjects and guarantee the confidentiality of the information, including removing identifying elements when legally required.
- **Data related to the Civil Registry:** when the processing involves data covered by this legal exception, respecting the special provisions that govern its access, use and circulation.

Applying any of the exceptions above does not release Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. from complying with the principles, duties and protection measures set out in current law.

In particular, when the processing involves sensitive personal data, health-related information or medical records, the institution must observe the special conditions of confidentiality, privacy, security, restricted access and other applicable legal requirements.

Providing information to authorities or legally entitled third parties is subject to verifying the competence, purpose, scope and appropriateness of the request, as well as the corresponding legal restrictions.

The institution will not interpret these exceptions as a general authorization to use, disclose or share personal data for commercial, advertising, promotional or other purposes not permitted by law.

The internal mechanisms for identifying, documenting and verifying that legal exceptions apply will be developed in the institution's personal data protection procedures.

## 4.7. Area responsible for handling queries, requests and complaints about personal data

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S., in compliance with Colombian personal data protection law, has institutional mechanisms to receive, manage and respond to the queries, requests and complaints submitted by data subjects, their successors, legal representatives or duly entitled persons.

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. will ensure that queries and complaints are handled within the time limits set out in current law, through mechanisms that allow them to be recorded, followed up, answered and documented.

While handling these requests, the institution will apply identity verification, standing, confidentiality and restricted-access measures, especially when sensitive personal data, health-related information or medical records are involved.

Data subjects may submit their requests through the institutional channels set out in [section 4.2.1](#421-channels-for-exercising-data-subjects-rights) of this policy.

## 4.8. Procedure for exercising data subjects' rights

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. guarantees data subjects, their successors, legal representatives and duly entitled persons the exercise of their rights to know, access, update, correct and delete their data, revoke authorization and other rights recognized by current Colombian law.

Queries and complaints related to the processing of personal data will be received, managed and answered in accordance with the requirements and time limits set out in Law 1581 of 2012 and other applicable provisions.

### 4.8.1. Submitting requests

Data subjects may submit their queries and complaints through the institutional channels set out in [section 4.2.1](#421-channels-for-exercising-data-subjects-rights) of this policy.

Requests may be made directly by the data subject or by their successors, legal representatives or duly authorized persons, as applicable.

The institution will verify the requester's identity and standing using mechanisms proportionate to the nature of the information requested, especially when sensitive personal data or confidential clinical information is involved.

### 4.8.2. Content of requests

To make them easier to handle, requests should include, as applicable:

- Identification of the data subject and of the requester, when they are different people.
- A clear description of the query, complaint or right to be exercised.
- The information needed to identify the personal data related to the request.
- Documents or evidence showing representation or standing, when applicable.
- A means of contact for receiving the institution's response.

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. will guide requesters when information needs to be added, avoiding unjustified requirements or disproportionate barriers to the exercise of their rights.

### 4.8.3. Handling queries

Queries about knowing, accessing or receiving information on the processing of personal data will be answered within a maximum of ten (10) business days from the date they are received.

When a query cannot be answered within that period, the institution will inform the requester of the reasons for the delay and the date on which the request will be answered, which may not be more than five (5) business days after the initial period expires.

### 4.8.4. Handling complaints

Complaints about updating, correcting or deleting personal data, revoking authorization or alleged failure to meet data protection duties will be processed in accordance with article 15 of Law 1581 of 2012 and other applicable provisions.

The maximum period for handling a complaint is fifteen (15) business days from the day after it is received, under the conditions provided by law.

When a complaint cannot be handled within that period, the institution will inform the requester, before the period expires, of the reasons for the delay and the date on which it will be handled, which may not be more than eight (8) business days after the initial period expires.

### 4.8.5. Incomplete complaints and corrections

When a complaint is incomplete, Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. will ask the requester, within five (5) days of receiving it, to correct the identified deficiencies.

If two (2) months pass from the date of that request without the requester providing the information, the complaint will be considered withdrawn, in accordance with the applicable law.

Once the complete complaint has been received, the institution will add a note reading "complaint in process" and its reason to the corresponding database, within a maximum of two (2) business days.

That note will remain until the complaint is decided.

When Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. is not competent to resolve a complaint, it will forward it to the appropriate party within the legal time limit and inform the requester.

### 4.8.6. Responses to requests

The institution's responses will be clear, complete, timely and consistent with the request submitted, within the limits set out in current law.

When a request to delete personal data or revoke authorization is not legally appropriate, the institution will inform the requester of the reasons that prevent it from fully or partially granting it.

Requests related to medical records and other confidential healthcare information will be handled under the special provisions that apply to their access, confidentiality and retention.

### 4.8.7. Filing complaints with the data protection authority

The data subject or legally entitled persons may file complaints with the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce for alleged violations of personal data protection law, once the query or complaint process with the data controller or processor has been completed, in accordance with article 16 of Law 1581 of 2012.

### 4.8.8. Recording and follow-up

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. will adopt mechanisms to record, classify, assign, manage, answer and keep evidence of the queries and complaints received, ensuring compliance with legal time limits and the confidentiality of the information.

The specific responsibilities of the institutional processes and the operating controls for handling these requests will be developed in the internal personal data protection procedures.

## 4.9. Validity, updates and retention of personal data

When substantial changes are made to the policy, they will be communicated to data subjects in a timely manner through appropriate and accessible means, in accordance with current law.

### 4.9.4. End of processing and disposal of personal data

Once the purposes that justified the processing have been fulfilled and the legally required retention periods have expired, Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. will assess whether the personal data should be securely deleted, anonymized or otherwise disposed of, in accordance with current law and its institutional procedures.

When a legal or contractual obligation to keep certain information remains, its processing will be limited to the permitted purposes for the corresponding period.

The institution will implement mechanisms to ensure that personal data is kept and disposed of securely, in a documented way and in accordance with the applicable legal requirements.
