## Introduction

Under Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 (article 17, item k, and article 18, item f) and Decree 1377 of 2013 (article 13), which set out rules for personal data protection, and in furtherance of everyone's constitutional right to know, update and correct information collected about them in databases or files, Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S., as controller of the personal data of its stakeholders —patients and their families, workers, contractors, students, paying entities, and inspection and oversight bodies—, obtained in the course of providing health services, commits to complying with these rules and protecting people's rights, and informs its stakeholders that it adopts the following policies on the collection, processing and use of personal data.

## Definitions

- **Authorization:** the data subject's prior, express and informed consent to the processing of personal data.
- **Database:** an organized set of personal data subject to processing.
- **Personal data:** any information linked or that can be associated with one or more identified or identifiable natural persons.
- **Semi-private data:** data that is not intimate, confidential or public, and whose disclosure may be of interest not only to the data subject but to a group of people or society in general. Its processing requires the data subject's express authorization. Examples: financial data and data about relationships with social security entities (health insurers, pension funds, occupational risk insurers, family benefit funds).
- **Sensitive data:** data that affects the data subject's privacy or whose misuse could lead to discrimination, such as data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political orientation, religious or philosophical beliefs, membership in unions, social or human rights organizations, or political parties, as well as health data, data about sex life, and biometric data.
- **Private data:** data that, being intimate or confidential, is of interest only to the data subject and requires express authorization to be processed. Examples: education level or merchants' books.
- **Public data:** data that the law and the Constitution expressly define as public, which may be collected and processed without the data subject's authorization. Examples: marital status, data in the national vehicle registry (RUNT) or in final court rulings.
- **Processor:** a natural or legal person, public or private, that processes personal data on behalf of the controller, alone or with others.
- **Controller:** a natural or legal person, public or private, that decides on the database or the processing of data, alone or with others.
- **Data subject:** the natural person whose personal data is processed.
- **Processing:** any operation or set of operations on personal data, such as collection, storage, use, circulation or deletion.

## Data processing policy

Since its founding, and by virtue of its corporate purpose, Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. has obtained and kept personal data from its stakeholders —hereinafter, data subjects—, which is collected, stored, organized, used, transmitted, updated, corrected and otherwise managed according to the relevant relationship (civil, employment, commercial or educational), applying the following policies.

### Channels for patients

To make it easier for patients to access their own information, the institution offers channels to:

- Review and update personal data.
- Request, review or change medical appointments.
- Check procedure results.
- Check service authorizations.
- Check account balances.

These channels are:

1. The patient portal, accessed from www.sefcb.co with a username and password registered by Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S.
2. Offices and locations: in-person service from our patient services staff, upon presenting an original ID document.

### Information security

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. is committed to the proper use and processing of data subjects' personal and sensitive data, preventing unauthorized third-party access that could reveal, compromise, modify, disclose or destroy the information. To this end, it has information security policies that include mandatory control measures.

### Data we request

The institution requests from data subjects the data needed to manage health risk and to fulfill the duties assigned by the regulations governing Colombia's General Social Security Health System. Providing sensitive information is free and voluntary for the data subject.

Except as provided by law, personal data may only be processed with the data subject's prior, express and informed consent, given in writing, orally, or through unequivocal conduct from which it can reasonably be concluded that authorization was given.

The institution will request from paying entities, workers and contractors the personal data needed to establish the relevant relationship (civil, employment or commercial). Providing sensitive information is free and voluntary; the data subject must give consent and authorization for its processing.

Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. will ensure respect for the fundamental rights of children and adolescents, observing the special requirements for processing their personal and sensitive data.

## Authorization

Processing personal data requires the data subject's free, prior, express and informed consent. As controller, the company has put in place the mechanisms needed to obtain data subjects' authorization, always making it possible for them to give it.

## How authorization is given

Authorization may be recorded by any means that allows it to be consulted later:

1. In writing.
2. Orally.
3. Through unequivocal conduct from which it can reasonably be concluded that authorization was given. Silence can never be treated as unequivocal conduct.

This consent procedure ensures that the data subject has been informed that their personal information will be collected and used for specific, known purposes, and that they may learn of any change to the information and of the specific use made of it, so they can make informed decisions and control the use of their personal information.

The data processing authorization (form MA0401-R1) states:

- Who collects the data (the company).
- What data is collected.
- Why it is collected (the purposes of processing).
- How to exercise the rights to access, correct, update or delete the data provided.
- Whether sensitive data is collected, and that the data subject may choose not to provide it.

## Proof of authorization

The company will adopt all appropriate and necessary mechanisms to keep a record of when and how it obtained each data subject's authorization.

## Privacy notice

The privacy notice (document MA0402D) is the physical or electronic document made available to data subjects to inform them of the data processing policies that apply to their personal data, how to access them, and the type of processing that will be carried out. It contains:

- The company's name and contact details.
- The processing the data will undergo and its purpose.
- The data subject's rights.
- How the data subject can learn about the data processing policy and any substantial changes to it or to the privacy notice. In all cases, the data subject will be told how to access or consult the policy.

## Duties of Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S.

1. Guarantee patients the full and effective exercise of their constitutional right of habeas data.
2. Keep information secure and private.
3. Use information for the institution's mission and for the purposes provided by law.
4. Handle patients' complaints about information in the database in a timely manner.
5. Not sell, circulate or exchange its patient database without a legal or contractual reason that justifies it.
6. Keep proof of the information given to the data subject and, upon request, provide a copy.
7. When requesting authorization, clearly and expressly state:
   - The processing the personal data will undergo and its purpose.
   - That answering questions about sensitive data or data about children and adolescents is optional.
   - The data subject's rights.
   - The controller's identification, physical or email address, and phone number.
8. Use the personal data of children and adolescents in a way that respects their prevailing and fundamental rights.
9. Authorization to process a child's or adolescent's personal data is given by their legal representative.

## Data subjects' rights

Data subjects have the following rights over their personal and sensitive data:

- To know, update and correct their data, including data that is partial, inaccurate, incomplete, fragmented or misleading, or whose processing is expressly prohibited or has not been authorized.
- To know why and for what purpose Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. collects information in its databases.
- To revoke at any time the authorization to keep their personal information in the institution's databases.
- To revoke authorization or request deletion when they believe processing does not respect constitutional and legal principles, rights and guarantees. Revocation or deletion applies when the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce has determined that the controller or processor has acted contrary to the law and the Constitution.
- To file a complaint with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce if they believe the institution has violated their right of habeas data.
- To request proof of the authorization given to the controller, unless it is expressly exempted as a requirement for processing under article 10 of Law 1581 of 2012.
- To access, free of charge, their personal data that has been processed.

## When authorization is not required

The data subject's authorization is not required for:

- Information requested by a public or administrative entity exercising its legal duties, or by court order.
- Public data.
- Medical or health emergencies.
- Processing authorized by law for historical, statistical or scientific purposes.
- Data related to the civil registry.

## Disclosure of information

Information meeting the conditions of article 13 of Law 1581 of 2012 may be provided to:

- Data subjects, their successors or legal representatives.
- Public or administrative entities exercising their legal duties, or by court order.
- Third parties authorized by the data subject or by law.

## Office in charge of requests and complaints

Requests, inquiries and complaints are handled by the Patient Information and Services office (SIAU) of Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S., which forwards each request to the area responsible for the data.

## Procedure for requests and complaints

1. Data subjects, or their legal representative, may exercise their right to know, update, correct, delete and revoke information by emailing contacto@sefcb.co, through the feedback (PQRS) menu at sefcb.co, or by sending a letter to Calle 19 No. 1-44, Barrio Blanco, Cúcuta, Norte de Santander, Colombia.
2. The request must clearly state what is sought —to know, update, correct, delete or revoke information in a database— and include the requester's contact details so we can respond.
3. Inquiries will be answered within a maximum of ten (10) business days from receipt, whatever channel is used. If this is not possible, we will explain the reason for the delay and give a new date, which will never be more than five (5) business days after the first deadline.
4. Complaints to correct, update or delete data will be answered within fifteen (15) business days from the day after receipt. If this is not possible, before the deadline we will explain the reason for the delay and give a new date, which will never be more than eight (8) business days after the first deadline.
5. If a complaint is incomplete, we will ask the requester to complete it within five (5) days of receipt. Once the complete complaint is received, a note reading "complaint in progress" and its reason will be added to the database within two (2) business days, and kept until the complaint is resolved. If two (2) months pass after our request without the requester providing the missing information, the complaint will be considered withdrawn.
6. Complaints are first handled by Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. If the response is not satisfactory, the data subject may turn to the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce.
