Servicios Especializados FCB S.A.S. processes the personal data of patients, relatives, staff, suppliers and visitors in accordance with Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 and its regulations.

As the owner of your data, you have the right to access, update and correct your information; to request proof of the authorization you gave us; to be informed of how we use it; to file complaints with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce; and to revoke your authorization or request deletion of your data where applicable.

To exercise your rights, write to contacto@sefcb.co.
