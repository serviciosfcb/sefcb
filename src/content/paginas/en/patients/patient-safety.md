Your safety comes first at every visit, test and procedure. Our patient safety program follows the guidelines of Colombia's Ministry of Health and Social Protection and the World Health Organization, and aims to prevent the risks of care with the active participation of our team, our patients and their families.

> **Patient safety policy.** Servicios Especializados del Corazón is committed to providing safe care focused on patients and their families. We promote safe clinical practices, timely management of care-related risks, and the reporting and analysis of safety events, to strengthen organizational learning and build a culture of safety.
