### What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and Saturday, 8:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. (Colombia time).

### When can I book an appointment?

In person, Monday to Friday, 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and Saturday, 8:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. Through the call center, Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., and Saturday, 8:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.

### What do I need to book an appointment?

It depends on your coverage. In general: a medical order, authorization from your health plan or insurer, ID and medical records. See the details in [booking an appointment](/en/patients/booking/).

### How can I book?

On WhatsApp at +57 320 936 2904, by phone, in the patient portal, by email to contacto@sefcb.co, in person, or by direct message on Facebook and Instagram.

### Is there priority service for private insurance, self-pay and special plan patients?

Our ticketing system classifies patients by their health plan and gives priority to private insurance, self-pay and special plan patients.

### How do I get my results?

In the [patient portal](https://app.sefcb.co/pacientes/ingresar?locale=es), from anywhere with an internet connection.

### Can someone else pick up my results or medical records?

Yes, with an authorization letter signed by you and that person, and a copy of your ID. Medical records are confidential (Resolution 1995 of 1999); without authorization we cannot release them.

### Where can I file a petition, complaint, suggestion or compliment?

Use the [feedback form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdNUpQtBaGXjQgKYLvxctA8vwbtnJvuMjyYgKuwYNLGUy0Jgg/viewform) (in Spanish), the suggestion boxes in our waiting rooms, or speak in person with the patient services (SIAU) coordinator. For compliments, we also keep an experiences book.
