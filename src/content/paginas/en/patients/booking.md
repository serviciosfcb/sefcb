## Ways to book

- **Booking desk on WhatsApp:** +57 320 936 2904. We will ask for your details and supporting documents: authorization, medical order, medical records and ID.
- **Patient lines (mobile and WhatsApp):** +57 320 274 5021 and +57 320 274 5025.
- **Main line:** +57 (607) 595 6075, option 1.
- **Patient portal:** request your appointment online at [app.sefcb.co](https://app.sefcb.co/pacientes/ingresar?locale=es). Our staff will contact you to confirm it.
- **Email:** send your documents to contacto@sefcb.co. For hospitalized patients, atencionalusuario9@sefcb.co.
- **In person:** Calle 19 No. 1-44, Barrio Blanco, Cúcuta. Take a ticket for appointment requests and have your documents ready.
- **Social media:** send us a direct message on Facebook or Instagram.

For **interventional procedures**, write to secintervencionismo@sefcb.co or call +57 312 362 1884.

## Hours

- **In person at our main location (Calle 19):** Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.; Saturday, 8:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m.
- **In person at location 2 (cath lab):** Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.
- **Call center:** Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.; Saturday, 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.

All times are Colombia time (UTC−5).

## Documents you need

- **Health plan (EPS) members:** medical order, original authorization, ID and medical records.
- **Private health insurance:** ID, medical order, medical records and authorization when the service requires it. If you do not have a printed authorization, we can process it by phone or through your insurer's website.
- **Special health plans:** ID, medical order, authorization and medical records.
- **Self-pay patients:** medical order, ID and medical records, if you have them.

You may ask to be seen by the professional of your choice among those available at the institution.
