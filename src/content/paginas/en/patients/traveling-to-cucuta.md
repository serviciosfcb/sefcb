San José de Cúcuta is the capital of Norte de Santander, in northeastern Colombia, on the border with Venezuela. It is the region's main financial and industrial center.

The climate is warm all year round, at about 320 meters (1,050 feet) above sea level. We recommend light, comfortable clothing for your visit.

## Getting here

- **By air:** Camilo Daza International Airport receives domestic and international flights. Check current routes with the airlines.
- **By bus:** the Cúcuta bus terminal connects with Colombia's main cities.
- **By car:** Routes 55 and 66 connect with Bucaramanga and Bogotá; Route 70 with the Caribbean coast; and the International Highway with the state of Táchira, in Venezuela.

## Before you travel

Book your appointment in advance and confirm with us how to prepare for your test and which documents you need. Message us on WhatsApp at +57 320 936 2904.

Members of our team can assist you in English. Let us know when you book.
