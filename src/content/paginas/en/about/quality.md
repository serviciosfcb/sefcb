## Certifications

Our management systems are certified by Icontec, with international recognition from the IQNet network:

- **NTC ISO 9001:2015, quality management system.** Certificate SC7166-1.
- **NTC ISO 45001:2018, occupational health and safety management system.** Certificate ST-CER961165.

## Scope of the quality management system

Outpatient psychology and nutrition consultations (low complexity); specialist consultations in cardiology, sports medicine and electrophysiology (medium complexity); invasive and non-invasive cardiovascular diagnosis (medium and high complexity); diagnostic and therapeutic support in cardiac catheterization and nuclear medicine (high complexity); and cardiovascular prevention and rehabilitation programs (low and medium complexity).

## Quality policy

Servicios Especializados del Corazón is committed to strengthening a culture of service through a management model that ensures patient satisfaction, with health services that are reliable, safe, timely and delivered with human warmth. We pursue excellence in care and continuously improve our processes for the benefit of patients and their families, staff and shareholders, to grow profitably and sustainably.

## Quality objectives

1. Ensure and increase satisfaction, timeliness and warmth in the delivery of services.
2. Provide health services that are reliable, safe, timely and delivered with human warmth.
3. Maintain staff commitment.
4. Ensure continuous improvement of the quality management system.
5. Increase the organization's profitability.

## Awards

- **National Responsible Hearts Award 2019**, from the Colombian Society of Cardiology, in the health provider category, for the Hearts in Motion program.
- **Business Merit Award 2019**, from Universidad Simón Bolívar, in the health company category.
- **Sustainable Environmental Management Award (GAES) 2019**, from CORPONOR, in the medium-sized service company category, for the SEFCB Palpita Verde program.
