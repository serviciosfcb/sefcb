Servicios Especializados del Corazón is a company from Norte de Santander created to provide health services, mainly in cardiovascular care. We bring together people, technology and management to offer reliability, good outcomes and the satisfaction our patients expect.

Our facilities allow us to care for patients from primary prevention to complex conditions, with advanced endovascular treatments, so patients do not need to travel to other cities.

We want the community, health professionals and organizations in the health system to find in us a cost-effective option for cardiovascular care.
