## Mission

To provide high-quality medical care to everyone who needs our services. Our purpose is to improve our patients' cardiovascular health and their overall wellbeing, enhancing their quality of life and future prospects. We are guided by empathy and professionalism, to deliver outstanding clinical care that ensures patient safety. We also recognize our responsibility to society and the environment, bringing medical excellence and environmental sustainability together.

## Vision

We aim to be a leading, accredited institution at the regional and national level, recognized for our medical excellence and social responsibility. As a cardiovascular health institution, we base our leadership on continuous innovation and comprehensive care, bringing together medical advances and taking on a dual responsibility: preserving our patients' health and a deep commitment to the environment.

## Corporate values

Our corporate values are the beliefs that unite us around our patients and guide what is right for the institution. They shape how our team behaves and acts, within a social ethic that helps us reach our goals.

- Service quality
- Empathy
- Reliability
- Social responsibility
- Innovation
- Integrity
- Passion
- Environmental sustainability
- Professionalism

## Corporate principles

Our corporate principles are the ethical traits that unite us around our patients and guide what is right for the institution. They lead us to act with integrity, within a social ethic that moves us toward our goals.

- Humanization
- Participation
- Transparency
- Commitment
- Adaptability
